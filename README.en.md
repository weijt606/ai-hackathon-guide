## Editorial notes

The Chinese edition is based on the author's six-part series, An Incomplete Guide to European AI Hackathons. It corrects obvious OCR errors and broken lines while preserving the original accounts, examples, and voice. Repeated covers and introductions are consolidated. Serial sign-offs have become chapter transitions, and some broad claims have been narrowed during editing.

The English edition translates all six chapters. Original source images remain unchanged in Chinese, so their wording may differ from the edited text. Page comments in Markdown map to the original pages.

When editing, update both the manuscript in `docs/` and the corresponding HTML. Keep the two language editions aligned.
